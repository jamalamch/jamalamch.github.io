1. HOW WE PROCESS YOUR DATA IN OUR APPS & GAMES

When you install or play our Apps & Games, we (and our partners) may collect and process information to operate the Services, improve your experience, measure performance, and — where enabled — deliver advertisements or validate purchases.

1.1 What information do we collect about you?

In most cases we do not collect data that directly identifies you (such as your full name, physical address, or phone number). We primarily process technical identifiers, gameplay data, and device information.

  • Device & technical data: device type, OS version, unique device or advertising identifiers (e.g. IDFA/GAID where available), IP address, browser or WebGL environment details, crash logs, performance metrics.
  • Gameplay & engagement data: session length, levels completed, scores, inventory or progression, feature usage, A/B test assignment, and similar in-game events.
  • Approximate location: country or region inferred from IP address.
  • Account / contact data (only if you provide it): email or other details you send for support, feedback, or optional sign-in (e.g. Apple / Google where a title supports it).
  • Transaction data (if applicable): purchase tokens, product IDs, and validation receipts from app stores — we do not store full payment card numbers.
  • Advertising & consent data: ad interaction events and your consent preferences for personalized ads where required by law.

1.2 Why and on what legal bases do we process your data?

  • Providing and managing Apps & Games (performance of a contract / legitimate interest): store progression, sync where supported, deliver content, keep the game functional.
  • In-app purchase validation (contract / legal obligation): validate store transactions and keep necessary accounting records.
  • Advertising and monetization (consent or legitimate interest where applicable): show ads via third-party SDKs or web partners, measure ad performance, detect ad fraud, manage consent.
  • Analytics and product improvement (consent or legitimate interest): understand player behavior, balance gameplay, monitor stability, forecast product metrics.
  • Stability and crash monitoring (legitimate interest): diagnose and fix bugs and crashes.
  • User acquisition / attribution (consent or legitimate interest): measure campaign performance with mobile measurement partners where used.
  • Customer support (contract / legitimate interest): respond to your requests.
  • Legal compliance & security (legal obligation / legitimate interest): comply with law, prevent fraud and abuse.

1.3 Who do we share your data with?

We may share information with the following categories of recipients, limited to what is needed for each purpose:

  • Service providers: hosting, cloud, analytics, crash reporting, and technical support partners.
  • Distribution platforms: Google Play, Apple App Store, Poki, Y8, itch.io, and similar portals that host or embed our games (they act under their own terms and privacy policies).
  • Advertising & mediation partners: ad networks such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or others integrated in a title; they may act as independent controllers for ad delivery and targeting.
  • Analytics providers: e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or similar tools.
  • iGaming / studio partners: when a game or slot experience is operated with a partner (e.g. Exa Gaming), necessary technical and gameplay data may be shared under that partnership and their policies.
  • App stores & identity providers: for purchases and optional sign-in flows.
  • Legal / regulatory authorities: when required by law.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Some advertising or analytics sharing may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” under certain US state laws; you can opt out as described below.

1.4 How long do we keep your data?

  • Gameplay / progression: generally while you use the Service and for up to about 24 months after last activity, or longer if needed to preserve purchased content while the game remains live.
  • Purchase records: as required for accounting and store compliance (often several years).
  • Support emails: for as long as needed to resolve your request and for reasonable follow-up.
  • Crash / analytics logs: typically shorter rolling windows (weeks to months), then deleted or aggregated.

When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it where reasonably practicable.
